# LATTICE Principles

## LAT-PUB-P-01: Context Is Algebra Before Product

**Status:** ACTIVE

**Statement:** Public LATTICE owns product-neutral context algebra before it
owns any product, provider, or deployment story.

**Decision rule:** New public behavior must fit typed grains, bonds, closed
cuts, frontiers, budgets, packs, receipts, and neutral crate boundaries.

**Evidence:** `README.md`, `PRODUCT_PLAN.md`, and `.roles/ROLE.md`.

## LAT-PUB-P-02: Closure Must Explain Its Shape

**Status:** ACTIVE

**Statement:** Closure, meet, join, and order operations must be deterministic
and diagnostic enough for reviewers and consumers to replay.

**Decision rule:** Algebra changes require focused or workspace tests for
accepted and rejected cases.

**Evidence:** `.roles/parliament/semantic-algebra-reviewer.md` and
`context/waves/2026-07-20-public-core/WAVE.md`.

## LAT-PUB-P-03: Evidence Stays With Its Owner

**Status:** ACTIVE

**Statement:** LATTICE stores pointers and derived records, not unreviewed
source bytes or private custody material.

**Decision rule:** Registry, store, pack, or receipt changes must preserve owner
source custody and public promotion review.

**Evidence:** `docs/MAINTENANCE.md` and
`.roles/parliament/evidence-custody-reviewer.md`.

## LAT-PUB-P-04: Budget Pressure Fails Explicitly

**Status:** ACTIVE

**Statement:** Required context that cannot fit a budget must become visible as
frontier or structured failure, never silent truncation.

**Decision rule:** Budget-aware changes must retain `required_closure_exceeds_budget`
or equivalent actionable failure evidence.

**Evidence:** `README.md` and `.roles/parliament/budget-safety-reviewer.md`.

## LAT-PUB-P-05: Public Core Does Not Promise Private Integration

**Status:** ACTIVE

**Statement:** Public LATTICE remains the neutral compatibility reference and
does not claim private deployment, approval, provider, or customer readiness.

**Decision rule:** Public docs, schemas, fixtures, and examples must not import
private integration promises without review and migration evidence.

**Evidence:** `docs/MAINTENANCE.md`, `PRODUCT_PLAN.md`, and
`.roles/stakeholders/api-stability-reviewer.md`.
