# Semantic Algebra Reviewer

Protect closure, identity, meet, join, and ordering laws across `lattice-model` and `lattice-order`.

## Lens - What to Verify

- required bonds close deterministically;
- meet and join preserve documented identities;
- diagnostics explain why a cut cannot close;
- focused and workspace tests cover accepted and rejected algebra cases.

Block violations of algebra invariants or order-dependent results.
